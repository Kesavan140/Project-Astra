Our Story

Building Excellence Since 1999

Founded with a vision to bridge the gap between strategy and execution, Astra Global Solution has grown from a small consulting firm into a global powerhouse serving clients across six continents.

Our journey has been defined by a relentless commitment to client success, continuous innovation, and the belief that every organization deserves access to world-class consulting expertise.

Today, we employ over 2,000 professionals worldwide, united by a shared purpose: to help businesses thrive in an ever-changing world.

Our Mission

To empower organizations worldwide with innovative solutions and strategic guidance that drive sustainable growth, operational excellence, and positive societal impact.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted global partner for business transformation, recognized for our integrity, expertise, and unwavering commitment to client success.
